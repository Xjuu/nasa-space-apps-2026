# FireSpot

Wildfire monitoring and experimental forecasting for responders, researchers, land managers and insurance analysts. The main surface is an operational map, not a marketing site.

Users define an area up to 25,000 km², inspect MODIS/VIIRS detections, run combined ML + physics forecasts and inspect time periods in the persistent player. All forecasts require both components. Keep daily ML guidance distinct from finer physical movement. Confidence is uncalibrated; scenario ranges are not confidence intervals. Do not invent accuracy claims or data.

Current work: reduce visual noise and bring forecast setup into immediate view while keeping every working control, keyboard interaction and mobile workflow.
